Who We Are

AeroBMSCE is the official aerospace, aeromodelling, and unmanned systems club of BMS College of Engineering, operating from Propel Lab-3. Established in 2012, the club brings together engineers from 10+ departments to design, build, and test advanced aircraft, drones, and autonomous systems.

What We Build

Competitive aircraft and UAV design

Drone racing and autonomy

Industry-grade fabrication and testing

Talent development through real-world engineering
